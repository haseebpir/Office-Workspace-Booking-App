## ✨ Overview

**DeskFlow** is a smart office booking and workspace management app designed for hybrid and flexible workplaces. Employees can book desks, meeting rooms, and shared spaces in seconds, while admins get full visibility into how the office is actually being used.

---

## 💼 Why Teams (and Clients) Love This

- 🪑 **Book a Desk in Seconds** — Reserve a desk or room from anywhere, before arriving at the office
- 🗺️ **Interactive Floor Plans** — Visual maps make finding and booking a space intuitive
- 📅 **Calendar Integration** — Syncs with existing work calendars to avoid scheduling conflicts
- 📲 **QR / NFC Check-In** — Fast, contactless check-in when arriving at a booked space
- 👥 **Visitor Management** — Simple guest registration and check-in for office visitors
- 📊 **Workspace Usage Analytics** — See which desks and rooms are used most (and least)
- 🔔 **Smart Reminders** — Notifications for upcoming bookings and check-in windows
- 🧑‍💼 **Team Scheduling** — Coordinate in-office days across teams for better collaboration
- 🛠️ **Admin Dashboard** — Centralized control over spaces, policies, and office layout
- 🎨 **Clean, Intuitive UI** — A frictionless design that requires zero onboarding for employees

---

## 🧱 How It Works

| Feature | Experience |
|---|---|
| **Find a Space** | Employees browse an interactive floor plan to find available desks or rooms |
| **Book Instantly** | A space is reserved in a few taps, synced with the work calendar |
| **Check In Fast** | QR or NFC check-in confirms arrival without any friction |
| **Coordinate as a Team** | Teams can see who's in the office and plan days together |
| **Optimize the Office** | Admins use real usage data to make smarter space decisions |
